## What he said (interview notes)

**Q: Us din koyla kyun khatam hua?**

> Us din thand zyada thi. Mujhe laga tha jitna roz bikta hai utna hi bikega, lekin shaam se pehle hi koyla khatam ho gaya. Kuch log wapas bhi chale gaye. Pehle se andaza lagana mushkil hota hai.

**Q: Kitna laana hai, kaise decide karte ho?**

> Main pichhle din ka sale dekhta hoon aur mausam bhi dekhta hoon. Agar thand zyada ho toh thoda extra rakhta hoon. Jumma ya chhutti ke din bhi kabhi sale zyada hoti hai. Koi fixed hisaab nahi hai, apne tajurbe se karta hoon.

**Q: Sabko ek hi rate dete ho?**

> Nahi, bhai. Jo regular customer hain unko thoda kam kar dete hain. Naya customer aaye toh normal rate batate hain. Waise bhi log bhaav-taav karte hain, paanch-das rupaye kam karne padte hain.

## The thing that surprised me

One interesting point discussed during the conversation was that customers purchasing kangris may also receive kani (small wood shavings) for free, particularly regular customers.

This is an additional part of the shopkeeper's service that a basic stock calculation would not capture. It also shows that customer relationships and small complimentary items are part of everyday shop management.

> Haan, kangri ke saath thodi kani bhi de dete hain. Regular customer ko kabhi free bhi de dete hain.

## Other answers worth keeping

- **When he runs out mid-day:** He may ask a neighbouring shop for stock; otherwise, customers may go elsewhere. Confirm the actual practice.
